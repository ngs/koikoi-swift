No account or sign-in is required. The app works fully offline.
Game Center is optional: leaderboards are only reported when the player is signed in.
The in-progress game is saved locally on the device and restored on the next launch.
Koi-Koi is a traditional Japanese card game played for points only; there is no wagering, in-app purchase, or real-money element.

- No purchased template, reskin, or third-party application code is used, and I have not submitted similar apps from any other account.
