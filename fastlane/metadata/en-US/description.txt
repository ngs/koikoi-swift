Koi-Koi, the classic hanafuda card game — with beautifully drawn cards and fluid controls.

Play two-player Koi-Koi under Nintendo rules against a search-based AI with four difficulty levels.

Features:
- Nintendo-rule scoring: yaku, koi-koi calls, dealer rotation and draws
- Four AI levels, topped by a determinized ISMCTS search engine
- Play by tap, drag & drop, or keyboard
- Yaku progress and "reach" (one card away) indicators make it easy to learn
- One match = one file: save games, resume and review anytime
- Game Center leaderboards
- iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro
